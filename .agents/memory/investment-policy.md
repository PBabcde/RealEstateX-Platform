The user approved aligning these four principles in the existing logic, calculators and dealroom checks before building any new screens:

1. Separate gross BAR from net rental return. The hard lower limit is 7.5% gross, not net. Use the same total-investment basis: acquisition price, acquisition costs, renovation excluding the kit, installed heat pump and installed PV. Count each cost once; estimated subsidies, future valuation and energy savings do not reduce that denominator.
2. Use a 5 kW heat pump plus glass-on-glass PV as the financial baseline. This is not a claim that 5 kW is sufficient for every building; keep heating demand distinct from the baseline.
3. Circle 1 is Gorinchem within a selected radius of 50–60 km, Circle 2 is Het Gooi / Randstad transition areas, and Circle 3 is Vlaanderen. Do not silently treat Circle 2 as Circle 1 or extend the primary radius to 70 km.
4. Reports and deal advancement must use the same current checks. A score alone or a previously stored approval must not bypass the financial, installation and regional requirements.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to align exactly these four points before adding screens after the comparison revealed conflicting definitions.

**How to apply:** Preserve these definitions across features. Treat incomplete legacy inputs as needing reassessment, not as approved. Geographic choices remain self-declared until a validated geographic source is connected; investment eligibility must not be presented as official WWS, legal or installation approval.
